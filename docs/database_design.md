# MASHome Database Design

## Project

MASHome – MASLD Knowledge Discovery Platform

---

## Purpose

MASHome is a biomedical knowledgebase for researchers, doctors, and students to explore information related to Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD).

---

## Core Modules

1. Diseases
2. Drugs
3. Genes
4. Proteins
5. Biomarkers
6. Clinical Trials
7. Targets
8. Bioactives
9. Patents
10. Medical Devices
11. Therapies

---
